Roll cleans up and searches the iPhone photo library entirely on the device. No account or login is needed.

How to review:
1. Launch Roll and go through onboarding. Allow Photos access (full access works best; limited access also works). The last onboarding step is the Roll Pro paywall (one-time purchase), which can be closed.
2. Home shows screenshots, blurry photos, big videos and duplicates. These work right away.
3. Describing a pile in words (Swipefiles tab, e.g. "receipts" or "photos of whiteboards") uses an on-device AI model. On first use Roll downloads the model (about 350 MB) from Hugging Face over Wi-Fi, then prepares it on the device ("Preparing search, first time only"). This takes a few minutes on a recent iPhone. Until it finishes, the other features keep working.
4. Swipe left/right to review a pile, then apply it to delete, move to an album or squish (re-encode to a smaller HEIC/HEVC copy). Deletions go through the standard iOS confirmation and land in Recently Deleted.
5. New in 1.1, Find: type a description in Ask Roll on Home, for example "beach" or a city and a year. The answer is grouped by moment. "Make album" shows a grid; Create adds only the selected photos to an album in Photos (it never deletes). "Keep this album updated" only offers new matches on Home; nothing is added without a tap.

Location (new in 1.1): to answer place words ("Lisbon 2024"), Roll reads the location already saved in each photo's metadata through PhotoKit (PHAsset.location) and matches it on the device against a list of place names bundled with the app (GeoNames, CC-BY 4.0). Roll does not use Location Services, does not ask for the device's location, and makes no network request for place names; the coordinates are stored only in the app's on-device database. Photos without a location still show up by meaning.

In-app purchase (Roll Pro, non-consumable, roll_pro_lifetime): search and album adds are free, and the first 50 cleanups (deletes and squishes) are free. Applying more opens the paywall, which offers one one-time purchase (no subscription). Restore Purchases is on the paywall. Purchases are handled by Apple through RevenueCat.

Privacy: photos, videos, locations, AI embeddings and typed descriptions are never uploaded. Network use is only the model download and RevenueCat for the purchase. The Monitor tab shows read-only device readings and does not change how the phone runs.
