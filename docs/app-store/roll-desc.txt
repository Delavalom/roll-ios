Clean up your photo library without sending a single photo anywhere. Roll's AI runs on your iPhone, so your photos, videos and the words you type never leave it.

DESCRIBE IT
Type what you want gone, like "receipts", "whiteboard photos" or "memes from group chats", and Roll gathers a pile for you with an AI model that runs on your iPhone.

SWIPE IT
Swipe through each pile: keep or let go. Roll learns from your swipes and gets better at spotting what you don't want.

GONE
Apply a pile in one go: delete, move to an album, or squish. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted, so you can change your mind.

READY-MADE PILES
• Screenshots
• Blurry photos
• Big videos
• Duplicates and similar shots, grouped by moment

SQUISH
Re-save big photos and videos as smaller HEIC and HEVC copies and see about how much space you'll get back before you start. Videos stay at 1080p unless you choose to keep 4K.

ASK ROLL
Search your library in plain words and find the photo you're thinking of.

MONITOR
Live readings of your iPhone's storage, battery and more. Read-only: Roll doesn't change how your phone runs.
