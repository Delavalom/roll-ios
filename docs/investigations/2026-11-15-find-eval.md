# Find eval: how accurate typed search is for trips, activities and things (2026-11-15)

This is the first judged eval of Roll's search for *finding* photos, not cleaning them up. Plan:
`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-15-find.md`. Decision: `docs/decisions/ADR-0006-find-search.md`. All numbers below come
from `evidence/2026-11-15-find-eval/`, which holds counts and shares only. The founder's 10 album queries appear there as
`private-01` to `private-10`.

## Setup

- **Library.** The founder's iPhone 13 Pro Max: 30,422 assets, 29,667 with an embedding, 18.6 % with a location.
  EmbeddingGemma 2 b70 (`file-99a7d34084f9186b-b70`, 256-d). The data is the phone's own Roll database and vector
  index, pulled with `-QAFindExport` (Debug only, read-only), plus Apple Vision scene labels for every asset.
- **Queries (49).** 37 real queries in six groups:
  - activity (6): soccer, gym, hiking…
  - event (6): birthday cake, wedding, Halloween costume…
  - place and trip (6): beach, snow, road trip…
  - thing (7): dog, cat, car, flowers…
  - dated (2): "christmas 2023", "beach last summer"
  - album (10): one query per trip or activity album, in the founder's words

  The other 12 are the nonsense strings from `evals/piles.json`. Public queries are in `evals/find.json`; the album
  queries stay in the private directory.
- **Labels.** For album queries, the album's members count as relevant. Everything else uses pooled judging, as in TREC:
  1. Take each variant's top 50 for each query and pool them.
  2. Show the pool on numbered 5×4 contact sheets of 256 px thumbnails.
  3. Claude judges each tile relevant, not relevant or unsure against a written rubric.

  Each new variant's new results were judged in a later round. About 4,100 thumbnails were judged in total. The judge is
  a single vision model, with no second judge and no human audit beyond the album labels.
- **Depth sampling.** P@50 says nothing about how big the answer is, so for every V0 answer the eval also judged 15
  random photos from ranks 51–200 and 15 from rank 201 to the end. That estimates precision across the whole answer.
- **Metrics:**
  - P@10, P@20, P@50 and nDCG@20.
  - Pooled recall@100.
  - Answer size (strong matches shown).
  - Nonsense refused, and false refusals of real queries.
  - Latency in the harness on the Mac.

## Results

### Today's search (V0) ranks well and answers far too much

| Group | P@50 | P 51–200 | P 201+ | Share of whole answer relevant | Median answer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| activity | .79 | .47 | .10 | .22 | 776 |
| event | .75 | .47 | .22 | .33 | 677 |
| place, trip | .65 | .16 | .07 | .15 | 635 |
| thing | .55 | .32 | .20 | .26 | 633 |
| album | .71 | .45 | .35 | .42 | 657 |

The first 20 are mostly right: P@20 is .823 overall. But the z ≥ 2 answer is about 3 % of the library (median 676
photos). Only about a quarter of it is relevant, and for some queries nothing relevant appears past rank 50 ("soccer").
You can't make an album from that answer as it stands.

### Where to cut the answer

These are V0 answers with depth-estimated labels. P and R are precision and recall of the answer against everything
relevant that the answer contains.

| Rule | P | R | F1 | Median answer | Median nonsense answer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| z ≥ 2 (today) | .27 | 1.00 | .42 | 941 | 738 |
| z ≥ 3 | .41 | .61 | .49 | 165 | 47 |
| z ≥ 3.5 | .54 | .47 | .50 | 89 | 12 |
| **score ≥ 0.6 × best** | **.56** | **.58** | **.57** | **130** | 165 |
| score ≥ 0.7 × best | .72 | .43 | .54 | 56 | 51 |
| top 200 | .51 | .63 | .56 | 200 | 200 |

A cut relative to the best score (`relativeCut` 0.6) gave the best F1 of the rules tested. It doesn't change the top 20
at all.

### Variants, four rounds

The table gives P@20 over the 37 real queries. In rounds 1–2 every pooled tile was judged.

| Variant | What | P@10 | P@20 | P@50 | nDCG@20 | Median answer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| V0 | today | .865 | .823 | .703 | .852 | 676 |
| V1 | embed "a photo of {q}" | .854 | .811 | .690 | .854 | 839 |
| C60 | V0 + cut at 0.6 × best | .865 | .823 | .700 | .852 | 130 |
| C70 | V0 + cut at 0.7 × best | .865 | .818 | .632 | .847 | 53 |
| V3 | events as a ranking bonus | .827 | .793 | .704 | .826 | 998 |
| V4 | Vision tags fused like words | **.884** | **.841** | **.724** | **.874** | 657 |
| V5 | 3 picked results as image seeds | **.897** | .837 | .724 | .867 | 333 |
| V2 (round 2) | dates and places as filters, first version | .714 | .674 | .588 | .703 | 657 |

V2 lost for three reasons. Each was fixed and measured again:

1. **Holiday words were removed from the text that gets embedded.** "christmas 2023" became "every photo from 24–26
   December 2023, in time order". It scored P@20 .2 even though all 34 relevant photos were among its 98 results. Fix:
   holiday words filter and stay in the embedded text (`keepHolidayWords`).
2. **Places were a hard filter.** Only 18.6 % of photos have a location (WhatsApp saves never do), so trip albums fell
   from .80 to .48. Fix: soft places (`PlaceMode.soft`). Photos located elsewhere are dropped, photos located there get a
   bonus, and photos with no location still compete.
3. **A bare holiday was a date.** "halloween costume" filtered to 31 Oct–2 Nov and lost every costume worn on another
   day (P@20 .55 → 0). Fix: a holiday filters only when the query dates it with a year, "last" or "this"
   (`bareHolidayFilters` is off by default; commit b4b0e5e).

Round 4 has all fixes in, and every pooled tile is judged.

| Variant | P@10 | P@20 | P@50 | nDCG@20 | Recall@100 | Median answer | Nonsense refused |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| V0 | .865 | .823 | .703 | .852 | .703 | 676 | 0 / 12 |
| V4 | .884 | .841 | .724 | .874 | .705 | 657 | 0 / 12 |
| V2s (fixed understanding) | .841 | .799 | .672 | .830 | .674 | 661 | 0 / 12 |
| V24s (+ tags) | .860 | .816 | .694 | .850 | .676 | 633 | 0 / 12 |
| **SHIP** | .860 | .815 | .673 | .847 | .627 | **69** | **9 / 12** |

SHIP is V24s plus the 0.6 cut, the nothing-found test, and event sections for display only.

**These P@20 numbers understate SHIP.** The scorer judges dated queries on their subject only, and V0 ignores the dates.
For "beach last summer", 83 beach photos are relevant by subject, but only 2 are from last summer. Scored with the
date window enforced:

| Query | V0 / C60 / V4 | SHIP |
|---|---|---|
| christmas 2023 (47 relevant in window) | P@20 **0**: answer 836, 27 of the 47 in it | P@20 **.95**: answer 23, 22 of the 47 |
| beach last summer (2 relevant in window) | P@20 **0**: answer 1,016, both in it | P@20 **.10** (the maximum): answer 4, both |

With dates enforced, overall P@20 over the 37 real queries is **SHIP .815, V24s .816, V2s .799, V4 .788, V0 .770,
C60 .770** (`round4/dated-enforced.json`).

P@20 by group (round 4, SHIP vs V0):

| Group | V0 | SHIP |
|---|---|---|
| activity | .86 | .85 |
| album | .80 | .81 |
| event | .85 | .90 |
| place, trip | .90 | .93 |
| thing | .69 | .71 |
| dated, date enforced | 0 | .53 |

How much of the answer is right (the whole strong answer, judged tiles only): SHIP has at least 42 % relevant and
shows 64 relevant photos on average, at a median of 104 photos per query (dated queries excluded). V0 has at least
11 % relevant and shows 71 relevant photos on average, at a median of 635. Unjudged photos widen the bounds: at most
75 % for SHIP, at most 90 % for V0. The V0 depth estimate above, about 26 %, is the better figure for V0.

### Nothing found

A query is refused when its best cosine is under 0.735. That refused 9 of the 12 nonsense strings and none of the 37 real
queries. The best cosine of the nonsense strings runs 0.694–0.741, so the margin is thin and tied to model b70. When
the app refuses, it shows the closest few photos instead of an empty screen.

### Speed

**Phone** (iPhone 13 Pro Max, 29.7k photos, Debug build with RollCore at `-Onone`, `-QASearchBench`, 4 warm calls
per query, medians). `FindConfig.shipping` per step (`FindSearch.Result.steps`):

| Run | understand | embed | pool | filter | read | rank | rest | **total** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| before the fixes (Low Power Mode **on**) | 0.1 | 9 | 16 | 37–76 | 1–17 | 51–56 | 54–75 | **180–214** |
| after 25312c5 (Low Power Mode off, thermal fair → serious) | 0.1 | 6 | 8 | 18–29 | 0.3–2.4 | 0.2–2.8 | 0–0.7 | **35–46** |

The power mode differs between the runs. Ask's typed search (V0) took 82 ms with Low Power Mode on and 43 ms with it
off, so roughly half of the drop comes from the power mode. The fixes account for the other half (from about
90–105 ms to about 40). The first find after a launch took 863 ms: it loads the tag index and warms the model. Nonsense
was refused in 35 ms. Model preparation after an install took 28–32 minutes.

The fixes (commit 25312c5) left results unchanged:

- **rest:** members are built once per library change, not on every query.
- **rank:** a running top 50 replaces sorting all ~30k z.
- **read:** SQL runs for the answer only, after ranking.
- **filter:** a lenient pre-check skips the date and place verdict for photos that can't qualify.

**Mac** (`FindSpeedBench`, `ROLL_BENCH=1`, a random-vector world with events and tags, loaded machine): Debug
113–264 ms → 40–125 ms. Release, best of three runs: 2.4–23 ms (one run before: 9–82 ms). The App Store build is
Release, so a phone running it should be well under the Debug numbers above.

## What's weak

- **Things are the weakest group:** P@20 .71. "cat" scores .20–.25 and "my dog" .55. This library has few cats, so
  the near misses outrank them. "flowers" scores .50. Tags help a little (+.03). V5 "more like these" seeds help most at
  the top (P@10 .897), so the app offers them.
- **Recall@100 drops with the cut:** SHIP .626 vs V0 .703. The cut trades the long tail for an answer you can turn
  into an album. "christmas 2023" finds 22 of 47 with the cut and 34 of 47 without it (V24s).
- **One library, one judge.** The thresholds `relativeCut` 0.6, `minTopCosine` 0.735 and `placeBonus` 1 were all
  tuned on this one library. Check them on more judged libraries before trusting them further.
- **Only two dated queries.** The date path is tested by unit tests, and on this library by two queries only.

## Reproduce

```sh
cd scripts/eval
ROLL_GEMMA_PATH=…/embeddinggemma-coreml/swift swift build -c release -j 4 --product find-eval
B=.build/out/Products/Release/find-eval; R=~/egcm-evidence-raw/2026-11-15/find
$B pool --db $R/db/roll.sqlite --index $R/index/vectors.egix --qvecs $R/qvecs.json \
  --queries ../../evals/find.json $R/find-private.json --export $R/export/assets.jsonl \
  --vision $R/export/vision.jsonl --labels $R/labels --variants V0,C60,V4,V2s,V24s,SHIP --out $R/round4
$B score --pool-root $R/round4 --labels $R/labels --queries ../../evals/find.json $R/find-private.json \
  --export $R/export/assets.jsonl --out $R/scores/round4
```
