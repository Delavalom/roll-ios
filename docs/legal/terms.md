## 7. Your photos and what Roll changes

Roll only changes your photo library when you tell it to:

- **Deleting.** When you apply a cleanup, Roll asks iOS to delete the items you chose, and iOS asks you to confirm.
  Deleted items go to the Recently Deleted album in Photos, where you can recover them for about 30 days before iOS
  removes them for good.
- **Moving to albums.** Roll adds the items you chose to the album you picked.
- **Squishing.** Roll saves a smaller copy of the photo or video you chose to your library and then asks iOS to
  delete the original, which you confirm. The original goes to Recently Deleted like any other deletion. The copy
  is re-encoded, so it may look slightly different from the original, and some details, such as captions,
  keywords and people tags you added by hand, may not be kept; Roll tells you before you squish.

These are your decisions. Roll's suggestions (what looks like a duplicate, blurry, a screenshot or worth squishing)
come from automated on-device analysis and can be wrong, so check before you confirm. Sizes and savings shown in the
app are estimates. Keep your own backup of anything you can't afford to lose; we are not responsible for photos or
videos you delete or replace, including after they leave Recently Deleted.
