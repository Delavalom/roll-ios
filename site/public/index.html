Roll: Private AI Photo Cleaner

Roll helps you clean up your iPhone photo library: it finds duplicates, screenshots, blurry shots and big videos you may want to delete, move to an album or shrink ("squish"). All of the analysis runs on your iPhone, using an AI model on the device and Apple's on-device frameworks. Your photos, videos and the descriptions you type are never uploaded. There are no analytics, no tracking and no accounts.
