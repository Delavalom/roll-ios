Roll Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Roll is a photo cleanup app for iPhone made by Luis Angel Arvelo Perez ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Roll. The short version: your photos are analyzed on your iPhone and are never uploaded. We don't run analytics, we don't track you, and we don't have accounts.

Your photos stay on your iPhone

Roll asks for access to your photo library so it can find things you may want to delete, move to an album or shrink ("squish"). All of that analysis runs on your device:

  • Roll reads your photos and videos through Apple's Photos framework and analyzes them on your iPhone, using an AI model that runs on the device and Apple's on-device Vision and Sensitive Content Analysis frameworks.
  • No photos, videos, thumbnails, embeddings (the numbers the AI model computes from a photo), or the descriptions you type are sent to us or to anyone else.
  • If you use iCloud Photos, Roll may ask Photos to download the full-size original of an item from your own iCloud Photos library so it can be analyzed or squished. That download is between your device and Apple, as part of iCloud Photos.

What Roll stores on your iPhone

Roll keeps its working data in the app's own private storage on your iPhone:

  • an index of your library: item identifiers, dates, sizes and types, and the results of the on-device analysis (for example blur and quality scores, image fingerprints used to find duplicates, and AI embeddings);
  • your swipefiles: their names, the descriptions you type, your swipe decisions, and small models trained on your device from those decisions;
  • a record of squishes in progress, so an interrupted squish can be finished or undone;
  • a count of the cleanups you have used on the free tier;
  • the downloaded AI model.

The AI embeddings and the AI model are excluded from iCloud and computer backups. The rest of the index (including the image fingerprints, your swipefiles and the descriptions you typed) is stored in a database that is not excluded, so if you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, it is included in that backup, which is stored by Apple or on your computer and not by us.

Everything Roll stores is deleted when you delete the app.

Network connections Roll makes

Roll connects to the internet for two things only.

Downloading the AI model. The first time Roll needs its AI model, it downloads the model files from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), which may serve them through its content delivery network. It downloads them again when an app update brings a new version of the model. Like any web server, Hugging Face sees your device's IP address and the request (which files are being downloaded, plus standard technical details such as the app name and iOS version that iOS adds to web requests). Roll sends nothing about you or your photos in that request. Hugging Face's handling of this information is covered by its own privacy policy.

Purchases. Roll uses RevenueCat to handle the Roll Pro purchase, and Apple processes all payments. When the app starts, shows the paywall, or you buy or restore Roll Pro, Roll's purchase component sends to RevenueCat:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID created on your device (it isn't linked to your name, email or Apple Account by us);
  • your purchase details from Apple (transactions and receipts);
  • technical information: the identifier for vendor (an ID iOS gives each developer's apps on your device), device model, iOS version, app version, preferred languages, App Store country, and your IP address as part of the connection.

RevenueCat uses this to tell Roll whether you have Roll Pro. Apple handles the payment itself under Apple's own privacy policy; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat's handling is covered by its privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No accounts. You never sign up or log in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. We don't track you across apps or websites, and we don't use the advertising identifier.
  • No selling or sharing of your information with third parties, other than the service providers above (Hugging Face for the model download, RevenueCat and Apple for the purchase) doing the jobs described.

Face ID

If you lock your Private swipefile, Roll asks iOS to check your Face ID or passcode. iOS does the check and only tells Roll whether it succeeded. Roll never receives your face data.

Children

Roll is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • You can change or remove Roll's photo access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos.
  • You can delete everything Roll stores on your iPhone by deleting the app.
  • We don't hold your photos or an account for you. To ask about the purchase records RevenueCat keeps for Roll, or to have them deleted, contact us at the address below.
