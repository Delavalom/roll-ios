Roll: Private AI Photo Cleaner

Clear space. Find your photos. Roll helps you clean up your iPhone photo library: it finds duplicates, screenshots, blurry shots and big videos you may want to delete, move to an album or shrink ("squish"). It also finds what you want to keep: type a trip like "Lisbon 2024" and Roll shows it grouped by day, ready to be an album.

All of the analysis runs on your iPhone, using an AI model on the device and Apple's on-device frameworks. Place names come from a list built into the app, so your photos' locations are never looked up online. Your photos, videos and the descriptions you type are never uploaded. There are no analytics, no tracking and no accounts.

Search and albums are free, and so are your first 50 cleanups. Roll Pro is one payment: no subscription, nothing renews.
